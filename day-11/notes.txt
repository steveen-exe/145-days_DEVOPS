Day 11 – Linux Storage Management (LVM) 
Today we'll move from traditional partitions to Logical Volume Manager (LVM).
 This is one of the most important Linux storage topics for DevOps, SRE, and Cloud Engineers
 because production servers often use LVM for flexible storage management.

 -it create a virtual storage pool that can grow srink / span accross multiple physical disk.
 without LVM->
Disk
 └── Partition
      └── Filesystem
#if the partition becomes full it is difficult to expand, may require downtime and somethimes it is impossible.
-the virtual storage pool thay is created by lvm that solved the problem 

Architecture ->
        Disk(brick)
        ↓
        Physical Volume (PV) - prepared brick
        ↓
        Volume Group (VG) - box of brickes
        ↓
        Logical Volume (LV) wall
        ↓
        Filesystempain on wall
        ↓
        Mount Point

PV -> Vg(PV1,PV2....PVn)-> top(Logical Volume) -> Mount to use as the storage for the users 
Don't allocate all disk space to logical vol. -> left some space for future allocations
